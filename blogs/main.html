Mechanical Brains

October 5, 2026

Absolutely ZERO parts of this is written by AI.

The below is a drawing of mine. I drew this. I DREW THIS, YOU BLUNT PIECES OF SILVERWARES!

I hate it. HATE it. AI is in everything now, in apps to draw, to apps to read, in booking flight tickets to booking a washing machine? I don’t care about AI. The only thing I could stand are LLMs, and not even very much so. People used to be people, have hobbies, and we were allowed to be bad at them. I used to be a graphic designer, a mid one at that too, but people still appreciated me because being more than bad in anything takes time and energy. But since the generative AI, everyone is treating everything as indispensable. They are treating ME like I can somehow create art in seconds, and modify it in time lesser than that. And that too the only reason people still ask designers for anything is because they KNOW the audience will not like it their content is generated. Profit incentive. And it’s JUST that. Why?

I understand that AI is supposed to make everything cheaper, but how much though? 10x? 100x? Completely free? Atleast now people avoid certain businesses because their posters look generated, but GenAI will obviously get better, smarter, “prettier”, and we won’t be able to distinguish between manmade art and a machine made one. What then? Everyone would just have to give in, no one’s word will matter. There will be no point in getting better at anything, because the AGI will be better than us. But what then?

Hot take, but I consider anyone who supports generative AI as SUBHUMAN. Waste of oxygen, waste of carbon, and a waste of space-time itself. Aww can’t take a pencil and draw a stick man? You piece of unworthy sewage wall? You picked the ONE thing that makes humans, humans; art. It is a disrespect to humanity itself if anyone considers AI generation - art.

It sickens my core that the low-lives which have nothing better to do than to generate images of itselves (yes, it.) as an 80’s pop star, or an astronaut, or blonde - live alongside us, AND can vote! And yes, the government doesn’t care about you or me, the ONLY thing they care about is how to make money. They WILL NOT STOP UNTIL THEY EAT THE LAST REMAINING MEAT IN YOUR ROTTING BODY. And you can’t do anything because, what can you do.

I DONT CARE about the new and improved AI features. Can write an essay better and faster than me? Very well. I bet it can’t replicate the feeling of making 20 grammatical mistakes in one single sentence, trying to sound Shakespeare, or be so bad at something, that just makes you feel good to have new experiences, or draw someone so bad, you can’t even recreate that masterpiece even if you tried… I don’t care about these new AI tools, or the NPU chips, or the software optimisations… maybe AI is the future, but I don’t want it to be mine. It would be my privilege to work in a field as a farmer for daily wage, than to do anything with genAI in my future. I would trade everything AI in my life for a second of happiness. Words can’t describe the hate I have towards AI.

I still dream of the days when I used to create just because creating made me feel human, somehow connected to this weird space rock. With now everything incentivised, it feels more like a reward that I created something without a black box feeding me slop. And I hate it. I hate myself for letting everything go to slop. And I HATE it.
